# Optimization Lab Exercises

1. Derive the gradient and Hessian of the quadratic objective.
2. Explain why coordinate minimization has a closed-form update for this objective.
3. Try a different starting point and compare the objective history.
4. Set an overly large initial line-search step and observe backtracking.
5. Change the sufficient-decrease constant and discuss its effect.
6. Add a stopping criterion based on objective improvement.
7. Generalize the objective to a positive-definite quadratic form in (n) variables.
8. Record both iteration count and runtime; explain why a low iteration count does not always imply lower elapsed time.
