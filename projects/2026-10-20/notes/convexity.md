# Convexity and Hessians

For a twice-differentiable function on a convex domain, a positive-semidefinite Hessian everywhere is sufficient for convexity. A positive-definite Hessian everywhere is sufficient for strict convexity.

For a quadratic f(x)=0.5*x^T*A*x-b^T*x, symmetric positive-definite A gives a unique minimizer satisfying Ax=b. Check symmetry and domain assumptions; positive diagonal entries alone do not prove positive definiteness.
